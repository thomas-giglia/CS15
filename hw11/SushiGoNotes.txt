SushiGo notes:

Deck has 108 cards:
14 Tempura
14 Sashimi
14 Dumpling
12 2Maki Rolls
8  3Maki Rolls 
6  1Maki Roll
10 Salmon Nigiri
5  Squid Nigiri
5  Egg Nigiri
10 Pudding
6  Wasabi
4  Chopsticks
(all will need to be pointers)

Coding up for 3 players 
(so 9 cards dealt per player)
- rest of cards will be in face down pile (will call it "deck")

Playing: Only 3 rounds (gracia Dios)
- all players simultaneously choose any one card from their hand to keep
(will make it in order, but won't show until afterwards)
All of these cards are placed face down in front of them (so removed from "hand")
After player 3 goes, reveal all three cards
After that, rest of hand is passed to the left (P1 -> P2, P2 -> P3, P3 -> P1)
- Face up cards (the one's put in the middle stay with the player)
NOTE: no drawing new cards, hands simply get smaller

Special Cards (only work when already played):
If a wasabi: the next nigiri played has 3x value
If chopsticks: Keep your first card, wait until everyone has done so, 
   - call "Sushi Go" and place another card, then all are revealed
   - NOTE: chopsticks are put back into hand before hands are passed

Ending a round: last card per hand -> left player face up in their pile

Scoring: 
Maki rolls: person with most rolls gets 6 points (int split if tied)
   - Second most gets 3 points (int split if tied again)
Tempura: 2 tempura cards are worth 5 points, 1 is worth 0 points
Sashimi: 3 sashimi cards are worth 10 points (any less are worth 0)
Dumplings: +1 point for 1st dumpling, +2 for 2nd, +3rd for 3rd, etc.
Squid Nigiri: Worth 3 points (unless on wasabi, then x3)
Salmon Nigiri: Worth 2 points (unless on wasabi, then x3)
Egg Nigiri: Worth 1 points (unless on wasabi, then x3)
Wasabi: Worth 0 alone (only acts a nigiri multiplier)
Chopsticks: Always worth 0
Pudding cards: kept multi-round (special!)

New Round: 
Announce scores from previous round 
Discard all cards, but pudding (into "discard pile")
Deal new cards for a new round from "draw pile" = "deck"

Ending the game:
Pudding: most pudding gets 6 points (int split if tied)
    - Least pudding loses 6 points (int split if tied)
Whoever has the most points wins, # of puddings is tiebreaker

Wasabi   Maki 1   Sashimi  Maki 1   Pudding  Egg N.   Squid N. Maki 1   Tempura
Maki 3   SalmonN. SalmonN. SalmonN. Dumpling Wasabi   Sashimi  Salmon N.Dumpling
